01 / Our program

More than
a uniform.

Baseball gives young players a place to learn, compete, and belong. The Bandits spirit is about showing up for your teammates, embracing the work, and enjoying the game together.

01

Build the fundamentals.

Every rep is an opportunity to grow. Hitting, fielding, throwing, and understanding the game start with attention to the details.

02

Bring the right attitude.

Effort, respect, and a willingness to learn matter on the field and beyond it.

03

Play for each other.

Celebrate a teammate's success. Pick each other up. Wear the Carolina name with pride.
